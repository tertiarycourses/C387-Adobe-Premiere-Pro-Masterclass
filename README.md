## About the course

This repository contains the learner-facing courseware for planning, editing, finishing and delivering video in Adobe Premiere Pro. The slides are concept-led and visual; detailed procedures, evidence requirements and acceptance criteria are provided in the Learner Guide and the lab guides. Every lab ships a valid Premiere Pro starter project with source and reference-output MP4 media.

## Learning outcomes

Learners will be able to:

1. Analyse and arrange footage in a logical sequence for editing.
2. Create editing logs and edit shots.
3. Resolve audio and still-image issues.
4. Collaborate with production teams to edit clips and colour.
5. Perform final touch-up, clean imperfections and deliver the video.

## Topics covered

1. **Get Started on Premiere Pro** — video theory, project setup, import, codecs, proxies, bins, logs and rough-cut planning
2. **Shot Editing** — Source and Program monitors, three-point editing, track control, trim types, pacing and transitions
3. **Audio and Graphics Editing** — gain and volume, keyframes, dialogue repair, music, sync, stills, graphics, effects and masks
4. **Clip and Colour Editing** — speed, time remapping, colour correction, scopes, shot matching, review and collaboration
5. **Final Touch-up and Delivery** — titles, captions, reframing, quality control, export, archive and production hand-off
